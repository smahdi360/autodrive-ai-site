Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

AutoDrive AI is an automotive content creation and publishing application.

Information we process

When a user connects a TikTok account, the application may process account identifiers, authorization tokens, profile information, publishing permissions, video information and performance data made available through TikTok APIs.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate the user, provide user-authorized content publishing features, retrieve publishing status and permitted performance information, and operate the content workflow.

Sharing

AutoDrive AI does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to service providers necessary to operate the application and to TikTok when the user requests TikTok functionality.

Retention and security

Credentials and authorization tokens are intended to be stored securely and are not included in public source code. Data is retained only as reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet applicable requirements and maintain security.

User choices

Users may revoke TikTok authorization through their TikTok account settings.

Third-party services and changes

Use of TikTok is also subject to TikTok's terms and privacy practices. This policy may be updated as the service changes.
